About this library

Who it is for, how items are described, and the design decisions behind it.

Type of library: academic digital library. Target group: DUT students and early-career researchers who have the work but not always the budget, the contacts or the time to go digging for sources.

The Threshold Collection brings together open-access items in four collections: research reports, peer-reviewed journal articles, theses and dissertations, and open textbooks. items are South African, and every collection has at least two, so local research sits beside international work instead of behind it. Every link and download was opened and checked before it was added. Where the licence allows it, a copy of the PDF (up to 15 MB) is stored on this site so the download keeps working even if the publisher's page moves.

Metadata

Each item is described with the fifteen-element Dublin Core scheme (we use thirteen of them). Open any item to see its full record.

ElementWhat we record
TitleFull title as published
CreatorAuthors, or the organisation for reports
ContributorSupervisors (theses) or editors (books)
SubjectKeywords used by search
DescriptionShort plain-language summary
PublisherJournal, university or organisation
DateYear of publication
TypeReport, book, open textbook, journal article, master's or doctoral thesis
FormatPDF, EPUB or HTML, with file size
IdentifierDOI or Handle, the permanent link
LanguageISO 639 code (en)
CoverageCountry of origin: South Africa or International
RightsLicence, for example CC BY 4.0

User interface principles

Simplicity

One search box on the home page, three collections, four menu items. Plain white background and one highly legible typeface (Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for low-vision readers).

Consistency

Blue always means Research, green Journal articles, purple Theses and dissertations and teal Open textbooks, on Home, Browse, Search and in every record. Every item has the same three buttons in the same order: Read online, Download, View metadata. The colours come from the DUT logo.

Feedback

Live result counts, matching words highlighted in yellow, the selected filter shown in dark, and a confirmation message when a citation is copied.

Visibility

The search box is the first thing on the home page, the menu never hides on desktop, South African work has its own badge, and every download button shows the file type and size before you click.

Accessibility

All text meets WCAG AA contrast on white, every control can be reached with the keyboard, and a "Skip to content" link appears on the first Tab press.

Built for ICTB301, Department of Information Systems, Durban University of Technology. Content, structure and design decisions worked through jointly by the project group.
